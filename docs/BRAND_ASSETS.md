# Store brand assets

The current blue “M” and “Maxey Inventory Flow” text are temporary prototype identity while the independent product name is decided. They are not an official Bob Maxey, Ford, or Lincoln corporate mark. The selected Home Store adds a small official store/manufacturer treatment and a restrained contextual accent color. A Ford logo is never used as the product or group-wide identity because the application also serves Lincoln and Fowlerville. See [Brand architecture](BRAND_ARCHITECTURE.md) for the separable product, dealer-group, OEM, and store layers.

The current locally bundled assets were retrieved from official dealership sites on August 29, 2026:

- Howell: `public/brands/bob-maxey-ford.png` from `https://www.bobmaxeyfordhowell.com/static/dealer-21521/logo.png`
- Ford Detroit: the same local Ford asset; the official Detroit file is byte-identical and came from `https://www.bobmaxeyford.com/static/dealer-21520/logo.png`
- Fowlerville: `public/brands/bob-maxey-fowlerville.png` from `https://www.bobmaxeyfordinfowlerville.com/static/dealer-23328/logo.png`
- Lincoln: `public/brands/lincoln.png` from `https://www.bobmaxeylincoln.com/static/industry-automotive/logos/supp/lincoln/transparent/lincoln.png`

These are locally bundled rather than hotlinked so a website path change cannot break the app header. Before external or public deployment, marketing should confirm the preferred high-resolution vector files and trademark/font usage. This internal prototype does not claim that the blue “M” is a Bob Maxey corporate logo.
