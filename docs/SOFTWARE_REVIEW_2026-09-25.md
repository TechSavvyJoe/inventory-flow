## Usability findings and implemented repairs

- **Inconsistent fact layout and excessive space.** Inventory and provider summaries mixed badges, paragraph fragments, and competing typography. Phone results now use a compact identity block, aligned location/hold/service facts, and one action row. Desktop results place AutoCheck, sticker, and OASIS in a short label/value strip. Material history findings remain prominent; complete facts remain in Records. The full Records workspace uses two report columns on desktop, one on phones, compact summary facts, and a single scrollable phone navigation row. Repeated Ford service summary text was removed while retaining every item.
- **Controls were uneven and some targets too small.** Primary actions now use restrained blue; secondary controls share borders, padding, typography, icons, focus states, and 44px targets. Across all eight synthetic vehicles, phone results measure 196–242px; complete desktop groups measure 220–257px at 1440px and 237–276px at 1320px. A separate long-equipment fixture exposed duplicate strip spacing: its narrow desktop group fell from 326px to 276px while keeping the complete package text. These replace the previously inadequate 600px phone/main-row-only desktop budgets. The first full report starts at 328px on a 390×844 phone, compared with 458px before this revision.
- **The starting inventory view made staff scan held or misplaced vehicles.** The landing filters are No hold, All, Held, and Offsite, with No hold the default. Known physical location and hold evidence govern this view; known visitors can appear at their reported store. Sold, loaner, transit, unknown-location, and conflicting-location records are handled conservatively. No hold is not an availability promise. Explicit VIN searches can still find and expose a vehicle outside this default.
- **Quick-view and service counts could depend on the selected facet.** Search matching now feeds independent facet counts, including matches at other locations.
- **Saving and customer preparation took extra navigation.** Rows expose Save/Saved. A reviewed customer summary and take-home page use an explicit vehicle-fact allowlist, preserving the boundary around private hold/customer/movement details. Nothing is sent automatically.
- **Lot lookup required typing.** Phone/tablet Scan opens the camera immediately. One view attempts VIN barcodes and stock-tag text; unique barcode matches enter search, while text matches require confirmation. Photo/manual fallbacks remain available. Desktop keeps ordinary search and hides Scan. OCR assets and image processing remain on the device, with no image upload.
- **Scanner interruption and modal handoffs.** Unrelated barcodes now leave scanning active. Workspace and scanner dialogs isolate the full application background; closing a vehicle opened from the shortlist returns to that exact opener. Nested take-home dismissal preserves the parent dialog's isolation and focus.
- **Remaining workflow density and keyboard gaps.** Action center task identities and actions are compacted; Equipment starts with its finder and results, with repeated specifications retained in a disclosure. Manager controls meet the 44px target. Employee editing restores focus to the initiating control when the editor closes.
